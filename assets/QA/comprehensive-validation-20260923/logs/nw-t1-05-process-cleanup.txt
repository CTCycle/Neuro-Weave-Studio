NW-T1-05 owned-process cleanup — 2026-09-23

The first official launcher run recorded backend/frontend process roots 10328
and 14504 in `launcher-t1-05.log`. Their run-owned process trees were stopped
with `taskkill.exe /PID <root> /T /F`; the commands succeeded and ports 6425,
6426, and 9849 were verified free.

The Codex in-app Browser inspection relaunched the official app. Its launcher
recorded roots 22396 (backend) and 8288 (frontend), both `cmd.exe` processes
started at 14:25:44. `Get-Process` confirmed those root identities. `netstat`
showed listeners on port 9849 owned by PID 31080, a Python executable under
this repository's portable runtime, and on port 6425 owned by PID 38992, the
repository's portable Node executable. Both listener processes started with
the launcher run. A normal `taskkill` attempt returned Access Denied; the
reviewed `taskkill.exe /PID <root> /T /F` calls then stopped both recorded
launcher trees successfully.

Final `netstat.exe -ano` verification showed no listening sockets on ports
6425, 6426, or 9849. No other process was stopped.
