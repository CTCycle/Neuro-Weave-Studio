### Recurrent case

The Builder materialized `Simple RNN`, enabled sequence and state outputs, connected the sequence and hidden-state ports to separate output consumers, saved revision 2, reopened the project, and reported no validation findings. The live payload records sequence output `[null, 4, 8]`, hidden state `[null, 8]`, two selected outputs, and a successful CPU Keras/PyTorch worker job `f9010788c0814ec0aab82274ee14325b`. Static validation and runtime both report 96 parameters.

Validation initially exposed a real implementation discrepancy: dedicated `recurrent.simple_rnn` and `recurrent.gru` nodes defaulted to the LSTM multiplier during static parameter counting, although their exporters/runtime selected their declared cells. The fix in `09d53a0` derives the cell from the dedicated definition IDs and adds Simple RNN/GRU/LSTM regression coverage. The final browser evidence confirms the Simple RNN static/runtime count now matches.

### Tensor-list merge case

The Builder materialized mathematical `Concatenate`, configured `axis=-1`, connected two tensor inputs to its variadic `Inputs` port, and connected the result to an output. After save/reopen, validation reported output shape `[null, 8]` and zero parameters; the CPU Keras/PyTorch worker job `de92be5de1b04731a5dd4c5851d77bee` succeeded with output `[1, 8]`.
