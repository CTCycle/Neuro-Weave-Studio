## Selection and scope

The current Model Builder ledger had completed the basic/advanced convolution,
recurrent, and attention slices. The next prepared Tier 3 slice was the seven
remaining Transformer definitions. This run covered the live catalog,
profile-aware materialization, selected parameters and edges, save/reload,
selected Keras/PyTorch and native PyTorch execution, the typed attention-mask
boundary, and invalid-parameter repair.

The official launcher used an isolated database/resource root:

```text
start_on_windows.ps1 -Action Launch -Backends tensorflow,pytorch,jax -Acceleration Auto -ExportTools -NoBrowser -ResourcesDir runtimes/cache/nw-t3-07-transformer-20260929
```

The backend health endpoint returned `{"status":"ok"}` and the frontend
rendered at `http://127.0.0.1:6425/`. The live metadata was TensorFlow 2.21.0
CPU, PyTorch 2.13.0+cu126 on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU, and JAX
0.11.0 CPU; TensorFlow JIT and JAX JIT were available, while PyTorch
`torch_compile` was unavailable.
